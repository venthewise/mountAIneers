    Survival

    Numbers on the cards below. This is what they mean.

    Energy
    Stamina. Moves cost a lot; rest brings it back.
    Food
    Eaten when resting or waiting. Run out for too long and you starve.
    Oxygen
    Matters from the South Col and the Summit (7,500 m and up). Each turn up there spends one bottle.
    Altitude sickness
    Builds in the death zone with no oxygen. Three strikes, and the climber dies.
    Icefall
    Camp 1. Tired climbers in bad weather can fall.
    Frostbite
    Storms punish lingering high with empty tanks.
    Season
    48 turns, about twelve game-days. If nobody is home with a summit tag by then, the season closes.
